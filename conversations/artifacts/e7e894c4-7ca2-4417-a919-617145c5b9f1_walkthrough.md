# User Profile & Smart Wallet Integration Walkthrough

The foolproof **User Profile Window** has been successfully integrated into the D-MEX protocol frontend. Here is a summary of the new capabilities:

## 1. High-Fidelity "Welcome Toast"
When you load the protocol page (and you have a saved Identity), you will instantly see an **iOS 26-inspired Welcome Toast** slide down from the top of the screen:
> 🟢 "Hi, [Your Name]. Welcome to the D-MEX"

The toast utilizes a silky, glassmorphic blur and retracts automatically after 5 seconds.

## 2. Interactive Profile Dashboard
Clicking the Profile Icon in the top right of the navigation bar now opens a robust, multi-tab modal:

### 👤 Personal Info
You can now input your First Name and Email. Clicking **"Save Identity"** stores this securely in your browser's local storage. This is what triggers the personalized Welcome Toast.

### 💼 Portfolio & Assets
This tab live-syncs with the protocol. 
It shows your DGLD, NFT Sword, and Commodity balances. 
> [!TIP]
> **Minted 3D Assets Persist!** When you use the "Mint 3D Asset" simulator in the Open Market, your Blender and Unreal creations are now saved locally. You will see them laid out in a grid under the "Minted UGC Assets" section of your portfolio.

### 💳 D-MEX Embedded Smart Wallet
To fulfill your vision of a **cross-chain, low-cost, customizable** wallet, I've designed the UI to showcase an **Account Abstraction (ERC-4337)** smart wallet approach.
The UI explains to users that they are using a gasless, native Smart Wallet (powered by ZeroDev/Privy), eliminating the need for complex browser extensions.

### 🛡️ Security & Privacy
Toggle your tracking preferences here. I've included switches for the **AI Guardian Behavioral Tracking**, **Anti-Spiral Circuit Breaker**, and **Zero-Knowledge Data Masking**.

## 3. Graceful Session End
The bottom of the profile contains a bold **"Sign Out & Disconnect"** button.
Clicking this triggers our deep-clean reset:
1. It disconnects your wallet (returning the protocol to "Guest" mode).
2. It completely wipes your locally saved Personal Information (Name and Email). 
*(Note: Your minted UGC assets remain in local storage so you don't lose the hard work of your demo!)*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To test this immediately, open the frontend (`http://localhost:3000`), click the Profile icon (top right), go to the "Personal Info" tab, enter your First Name, and click "Save Identity". Then refresh the page to see the new iOS animation!
