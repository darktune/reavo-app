## 1. Double-Spend Prevention / Atomic Respawn (WS3)
The protocol now actively prevents double-spends and ensures synchronized asset transfers:
- **Real-Time Event Listeners**: The Guardian Node connects to the Scroll L2 RPC and actively listens for `Transfer` (ERC-20/ERC-721) and `TransferSingle` (ERC-1155) events emitted by the D-MEX smart contracts.
- **Atomic Trigger**: Once a transfer to or from the `Vault Proxy` is confirmed on-chain, the Guardian instantly generates an `asset_spawn` instruction.
- **WebSocket Broadcast**: This instruction is broadcasted in real-time via WebSocket to all connected Game SDKs (e.g., the frontend), ensuring items are only "spawned" in-game *after* the on-chain transfer is verified.

## 2. Multi-Operator Threshold Logic (WS2)
We've introduced a simulated 2-of-3 validation model for high-risk operations:
- **Dynamic Decision Logic**: The Guardian AI no longer operates as a sole dictator. It evaluates the risk score:
  - If Risk < 50 (Low/Medium): The AI auto-signs and approves the transaction (`approve`).
  - If Risk >= 50 (High): Instead of auto-rejecting or approving, the AI flags the transaction with a **`pending_co_signer`** decision.
- **UI Integration**: If a swap triggers the threshold (high risk), the D-MEX frontend intercepts this state and displays a "Swap Requires Human Co-Signer due to high risk!" warning, perfectly demonstrating the multi-sig security narrative for your demo.
